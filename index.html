For family and caregivers

Helping someone on Rasonque

The skin routine is the part most people need help with.

  • Line up everything in the order it goes on: the antibiotic pill, the spray, two creams, moisturizer and sunscreen.
  • Apply the triamcinolone cream and moisturizer to the back and trunk.
  • Set two reminders a day, morning and evening.
  • Know when to call the care team.
